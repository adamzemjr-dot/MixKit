MixKit is a portable smoothie starter box built for modern life. Whether you're rushing to school, training after workouts, or just want better nutrition without the mess — MixKit makes it effortless.

What’s Inside

Every MixKit box includes a compact blender cup, long-lasting rechargeable batteries, and a curated recipe guide. Designed to remove friction between wanting to eat well and actually doing it.

Signature Blends

Balanced, delicious flavours made to fuel your day without slowing you down.

Strawberry Banana

A smooth, comforting classic that balances natural sweetness with a creamy texture. Perfect for mornings, recovery, or when you want something familiar and satisfying.

Mixed Berry

A bold antioxidant-rich blend packed with blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries. Designed to support focus, energy, and a refreshing boost anytime during the day.

Green Energy
