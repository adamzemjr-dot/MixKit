MixKit is the all-in-one smoothie starter box built for busy lives. It allows anyone to make fresh, delicious smoothies anywhere — no kitchen, no cables, no effort.

Designed for students, athletes, and people on the move, MixKit makes healthy habits easy to start and easy to keep.

What is MixKit?

MixKit was created to remove the barriers to healthy eating. Smoothies are nutritious and convenient, but they’re often expensive or require bulky equipment.

MixKit combines portability, power, and simplicity into one compact box, allowing users to blend fresh smoothies wherever life takes them.

What’s Inside the Box

Portable Blender

A powerful, rechargeable blender cup designed to crush fruit, ice, and frozen ingredients with ease.

Extra Batteries

Two backup batteries ensure uninterrupted blending, perfect for travel, school, or outdoor use.

Recipe Book

A detailed smoothie guide packed with classic blends, energy boosters, and recovery smoothies.

Endless Flavours

From strawberry banana classics to tropical mango blends and vibrant green smoothies, MixKit encourages creativity and experimentation.
