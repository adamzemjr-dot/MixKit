MixKit is a portable smoothie starter box designed for people who want real nutrition without slowing down. It lets you blend fresh smoothies anywhere — no kitchen, no cables, no hassle.

Whether you’re a student, athlete, or always on the move, MixKit turns healthy habits into something effortless and enjoyable.

What is MixKit?

MixKit was created to remove the friction from healthy eating. Smoothies are one of the easiest ways to get essential nutrients, but traditional blenders are bulky and cafés are expensive.

MixKit solves this by combining portability, power, and simplicity into one compact kit — making nutrition accessible wherever life takes you.

What’s Inside the Box

Portable Blender

A sleek, rechargeable blender cup capable of crushing fruit and ice, designed to be lightweight, durable, and easy to clean.

Extra Batteries

Two backup batteries ensure uninterrupted blending, perfect for long days, travel, and outdoor use.

Recipe Book

A detailed smoothie guide filled with classic blends, tropical flavors, and nutrient-packed recipes for energy and recovery.

Endless Flavours

From classic strawberry banana to refreshing green smoothies and tropical mango blends, MixKit encourages creativity and experimentation.
