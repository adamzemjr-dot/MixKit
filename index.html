MixKit is a portable smoothie starter box designed for busy people who want real nutrition without the hassle. It allows anyone to blend fresh smoothies anywhere — no kitchen required.

From school mornings to gym sessions and travel days, MixKit turns healthy habits into something effortless.

What is MixKit?

MixKit was created to solve a growing problem: healthy food is often inconvenient. Smoothies are one of the easiest ways to consume fruit and nutrients, but traditional blenders are bulky and cafés are expensive.

MixKit combines portability, simplicity, and power into one compact solution that fits seamlessly into everyday life.

What’s Inside the Box

Portable Blender

A compact yet powerful blender cup capable of blending fruit, ice, and frozen ingredients smoothly and efficiently.

Extra Batteries

Two rechargeable backup batteries ensure MixKit is always ready, even on long days or when traveling.

Recipe Book

A detailed guide packed with smoothie recipes ranging from classic strawberry banana to tropical and green blends.

Endless Flavours

MixKit encourages creativity. Users can explore flavors, textures, and nutritional combinations to match their lifestyle and goals.

The Pitch

The wellness market continues to grow, but convenience remains the biggest challenge. MixKit solves this by making nutrition portable, affordable, and easy to maintain.

With low production costs, strong branding potential, and a wide target audience, MixKit is designed to scale.
