MixKit is a portable smoothie starter box built for modern life. Whether you're rushing to school, training after workouts, or just want better nutrition without the mess — MixKit makes it effortless.

From classic strawberry banana blends to tropical and green smoothies, MixKit gives users the freedom to experiment and personalise their nutrition.

Strawberry Banana

A smooth and comforting classic combining ripe strawberries with creamy banana. Naturally sweet, satisfying, and perfect for mornings or post-workout recovery.

Mixed Berry

A vibrant antioxidant-rich blend packed with blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries. Bold flavour with a refreshing boost of energy and focus.

Green Energy

A clean, balanced smoothie featuring spinach, apple, and citrus notes. Light, refreshing, and designed for sustained energy throughout the day.
