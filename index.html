MixKit is a portable smoothie starter box built for people who want real nutrition without slowing down their day.

Whether you're heading to class, training after a workout, or just trying to eat better without the mess, MixKit removes the friction. No prep. No cleanup. Just blend and go.

What’s Inside

Every MixKit box includes a compact blending cup designed for daily use, long-lasting rechargeable batteries, and a curated recipe guide that takes the guesswork out of nutrition.

The system is intentionally simple — fewer steps, fewer decisions, and less time between you and something genuinely good for your body.

Signature Blends

Carefully balanced flavours designed to fuel your day, support your energy, and actually taste good.

Strawberry Banana

A smooth, comforting classic that balances natural sweetness with a creamy texture. Ideal for mornings, recovery, or whenever you want something familiar and satisfying.

Mixed Berry

A bold blend packed with antioxidants from blueberries, raspberries, and blackberries. Designed to support focus, clarity, and steady energy throughout the day.

Green Energy

A clean, refreshing option featuring greens balanced with apple and citrus notes. Light, hydrating, and made for sustained performance without the crash.

The Pitch

MixKit exists to close the gap between wanting to eat well and actually doing it. By removing preparation, cleanup, and decision fatigue, healthy habits become easier to maintain.

It’s not about perfection — it’s about consistency, simplicity, and making better choices feel effortless.
